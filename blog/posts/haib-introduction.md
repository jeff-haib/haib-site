# Introducing HAIB

HAIB stands for Human AI Interaction Bridge.

We build practical AI tools for professionals, creators, and teams who work with AI in everyday workflows. The focus is simple: make useful products, keep the experience clear, and help people move from scattered AI usage to repeatable work.

Our first product areas include credential visibility, conversation capture, language learning, video workflow automation, and workflow orchestration.
